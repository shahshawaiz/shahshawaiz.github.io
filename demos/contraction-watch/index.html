Watch not in the list?
  • Turn sharing off and on again. Some models only share while a workout is running.
  • Close other apps that are reading heart rate from the watch.
  • On a Mac: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Bluetooth, allow Chrome.
  • iPhone browsers cannot do this. Use Chrome on a computer or Android phone.
  • If the watch has no sharing option, this demo cannot read it. Use tap timing.
